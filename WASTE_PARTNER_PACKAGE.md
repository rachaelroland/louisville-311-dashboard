## Strategic Context: MyLouisville Multi-Channel Ecosystem

Louisville Metro offers waste services through **multiple channels**:

### 1. **Metro311 Mobile App** ([Louisville Metro311](https://louisvilleky.gov/government/metro311/services/download-metro311-app))
- Schedule large item pickups
- Request waste containers (new, damaged, stolen)
- Report missed collections
- Find collection schedule by address
- Track service request status

### 2. **Recycle Coach App**
- Personalized collection schedule
- Item-specific disposal guidance
- Holiday schedule alerts
- Reminders

### 3. **Phone/311 Call Center** (7 AM - 7 PM, Mon-Fri)
- Residents without smartphones (20-30% of population)
- Immediate answers to informational questions
- Complex situations requiring human guidance
- Accessibility for elderly/low-tech-literacy residents

### 4. **Website** ([LouisvilleKY.gov](https://louisvilleky.gov))
- Comprehensive policy information
- Facility locations and hours
- Service descriptions

## The Gap: Informational Questions Not Served by Apps

**Apps excel at transactions** (request, report, schedule), but **residents also need information**:

- Where is the Haz Bin? What are the hours?
- Is there pickup on Thanksgiving? When will my delayed pickup be?
- What's the weight limit for my cart?
- How do I properly dispose of paint/batteries/electronics?
- Can I put my cart out the night before?

**These informational questions generate thousands of 311 calls** because:
- Apps don't answer "Where/When/How/What" questions well
- Residents call 311 expecting immediate answers
- Not everyone downloads or uses mobile apps
- Some questions come up urgently (holiday week, cart problems)

**This is where the AI assistant adds value** - answering informational questions 24/7 for residents who call 311, complementing the app's transactional capabilities.

## Updated Q&A Examples (Multi-Channel Aware)

### Example 1: Cart Request (Transactional + Informational)
**Q:** "How do I get a trash cart?"
**A:** "Trash carts are provided FREE to residents in Louisville's Urban Services District. You can request a cart using the **Metro311 mobile app** (select 'Waste Container Request' > 'New') or by **calling 311**. The standard size is 95 gallons. Your cart will be delivered within 7-10 business days. The cart remains city property."

*Note: Mentions both app and phone options*

### Example 2: Hazardous Waste (Informational Only)
**Q:** "Where can I dispose of hazardous waste in Louisville?"
**A:** "Take household hazardous waste to the **Haz Bin at 7501 Grade Lane**. Hours: Tuesday-Saturday, 9:30 AM - 4:00 PM. It's FREE for Louisville/Jefferson County residents. Accepted items include: paint, motor oil, batteries, gasoline, cleaners, pesticides, fire extinguishers, fluorescent light bulbs, chemicals, antifreeze, and more. Do NOT put hazardous waste in your regular trash."

*Note: Pure information answer - perfect for AI*

### Example 3: Holiday Schedule (Informational + App Reference)
**Q:** "What happens to my trash schedule when there's a holiday?"
**A:** "When a Metro holiday falls during the week, all trash, recycling, and yard waste collections are delayed by ONE DAY for the rest of that week. For example, if Monday is a holiday, Monday's route runs on Tuesday, Tuesday's route runs on Wednesday, and so on. Download the free **Recycle Coach app** to get automatic schedule updates and reminders. You can also call 311 to confirm your adjusted schedule."

*Note: Provides answer immediately, mentions app for ongoing tracking*
