## Sample New Q&A Pairs (Critical Priority)

### Example 1: Cart Management
**Q:** How do I get a trash cart?
**A:** Trash carts are provided FREE to residents in Louisville's Urban Services District. If you're a new resident or need a cart, call 311 and request "Waste Container Request - New." The standard size is 95 gallons. Your cart will be delivered to your address within 7-10 business days.

**Q:** How do I get a recycling cart?
**A:** Call 311 and request a recycling cart by selecting "Waste Container Request" then "New." The standard size is 95 gallons and is FREE for residents in the Urban Services District. You can also use the Recycle Coach app or visit louisvilleky.gov/311 to request online.

**Q:** My trash cart is damaged or broken. How do I get it replaced?
**A:** Call 311 to report a damaged cart and request a replacement. Describe the damage (cracked body, broken wheels, damaged lid, etc.). Metro will schedule a replacement cart delivery, typically within 7-10 business days. The cart is city property, so replacement is FREE.

**Q:** My trash cart was stolen. What should I do?
**A:** Call 311 immediately to report the stolen cart and request a replacement. You may also want to file a police report (call non-emergency: 502-574-5673). Metro will provide a replacement cart FREE of charge within 7-10 business days.

### Example 2: Holiday Schedule
**Q:** Is there trash pickup on holidays?
**A:** No, there is NO waste collection on Metro Government holidays. These holidays are: New Year's Day, Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, and Christmas Day.

**Q:** What happens to my trash schedule when there's a holiday?
**A:** When a Metro holiday falls during the week, all trash, recycling, and yard waste collections are delayed by ONE DAY for the rest of that week. For example, if Monday is a holiday, Monday's route runs on Tuesday, Tuesday's route runs on Wednesday, and so on. Use the Recycle Coach app for automatic schedule updates.

### Example 3: Hazardous Waste
**Q:** Where can I dispose of hazardous waste in Louisville?
**A:** Take household hazardous waste to the **Haz Bin** at 7501 Grade Lane. Hours: Tuesday-Saturday, 9:30 AM - 4:00 PM. It's FREE for Louisville/Jefferson County residents. Accepted items: paint, motor oil, batteries, gasoline, cleaners, pesticides, fire extinguishers, light bulbs, chemicals, antifreeze, and more. Do NOT put these in your regular trash.

**Q:** How do I dispose of paint?
**A:** **Oil-based paint or lead paint:** Take to the Haz Bin (7501 Grade Lane, Tue-Sat 9:30 AM-4 PM, FREE). **Latex paint:** Can go in your regular trash ONLY if the paint is completely dry. To dry latex paint, remove the lid, add kitty litter or sand, let it harden, then place in trash with the lid off so collectors can see it's dry.
