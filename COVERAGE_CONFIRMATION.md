### ✅ 2. "Is this address eligible for city-provided sanitation service?"

**Status:** COVERED (NEW)
**Question ID:** waste_eligibility_001
**Question Text:** "Is this address eligible for city-provided sanitation service?"

**Answer Includes:**
- Urban Services District requirement
- How to check eligibility: MyLouisville app feature, website lookup, call 311
- Eligible property types: single-family homes, buildings with ≤8 units
- What to do if outside service area: contact city administrator or arrange private collection

**Source Pages:**
- https://louisvilleky.gov/government/public-works/services/garbage-collection
- https://louisvilleky.gov/government/metro-technology-services/mylouisville
- Louisville KY Urban Service District data

### ✅ 3. "How do I report a missed trash, recycling, or yard waste pickup?"

**Status:** COVERED (ENHANCED)
**Question ID:** waste_missed_001
**Question Text:** "How do I report a missed trash, recycling, or yard waste pickup?"

**Answer Includes:**
- All three reporting channels: Metro311 app (specific navigation), phone (311 or 574-5000), online

- Response time: 72 hours for collection
- Troubleshooting reminder: cart placement requirements

### ✅ 5. "Which city service/department handles my trash, recycling, or yard waste?"

**Status:** COVERED (NEW)
**Question ID:** waste_department_001
**Question Text:** "Which city service or department handles my trash, recycling, or yard waste?"

**Answer Includes:**
- Official department name: Louisville Metro Public Works - Solid Waste Management Services Division
- Scope: all residential garbage, recycling, yard waste in Urban Services District
- Contact methods: Metro311 (phone, app, online)
- Related facilities: Haz Bin, Waste Reduction Center

**Source Pages:**
- https://louisvilleky.gov/government/public-works
- https://louisvilleky.gov/government/public-works/solid-waste-management-services

### ✅ 6. "Where can I find forms related to sanitation or waste services?"

**Status:** COVERED (NEW)
**Question ID:** waste_forms_001
**Question Text:** "Where can I find forms related to sanitation or waste services?"

**Answer Includes:**
- Key insight: Most services don't require paper forms - use Metro311 system instead
- Digital options: Metro311 app, phone (311), website (louisvilleky.gov/311)
- Special case: Waste Set-Out Variance for residents needing assistance (requires doctor's note/photos, annual renewal)
- Resource page: louisvilleky.gov/government/public-works/solid-waste-management-services
