## The Problem

We're missing **90% of waste management questions**. Three critical gaps:

### 1. Hazardous Waste - SAFETY CRITICAL (0% coverage)
- Residents don't know where to dispose paint, batteries, chemicals, electronics
- Risk of improper disposal causing environmental/health hazards
- Haz Bin facility exists but completely absent from AI system

### 2. Cart Management - HIGH VOLUME (0% coverage)
- Zero coverage of getting, replacing, or managing trash/recycling carts
- Estimated 25-30% of all waste calls are cart-related
- Simple questions taking 5-10 minutes from operators

### 3. Holiday Schedules - TIME SENSITIVE (0% coverage)
- No information on holiday pickup schedules or delays
- Call volume spikes 300-500% during holiday weeks
- Predictable, preventable resident frustration
